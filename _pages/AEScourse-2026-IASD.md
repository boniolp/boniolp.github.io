<h2>IASD Master: Data acquisition, extraction, and storage</h2>

<p class="aes-lead">The objective of this course is to present the principles of the knowledge discovery and data mining pipeline, covering acquisition, storage, and extraction. From storing data to searching for similarities, the course will include a mix of lectures and practical sessions.</p>

<h3 id="lectures">Lectures</h3>

<div class="aes-note">Each lecture has two 1.5&nbsp;h parts (with a 15&nbsp;min break in between), starts at 9&nbsp;am and ends at 12:15&nbsp;pm. For each lecture (except for lab days), I provide a Q&amp;A session to serve as a recap and a technical dive into what we discussed during the corresponding lecture. <b>Click on a lecture title</b> to see its abstract and key topics.</div>

<div class="aes-title">Multi-dimensional similarity search</div>

<div class="aes-body">
Similarity search as a core extraction task: given a query, find the most similar objects in a collection. We define distance measures (Euclidean, elastic distances such as DTW), dimensionality reduction (PAA, APCA, SAX), and the lower-bounding principle that enables exact search without scanning everything. The second part of the session is the second practice session (Lab 2).</p>
<ul class="aes-tags"><li>Nearest-neighbour queries</li><li>Euclidean distance</li><li>Dynamic Time Warping</li><li>PAA / APCA</li><li>Lower bounds</li></ul>
</div>

<ul class="aes-tags"><li>k-d trees</li><li>R-trees &amp; MBRs</li><li>MINDIST</li><li>Best-first search</li><li>Curse of dimensionality</li><li>Time series indexes</li></ul>
</div>
